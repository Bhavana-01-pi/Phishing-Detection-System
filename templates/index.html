About PhishDetector

Phishing is an internet scam in which an attacker sends out fake messages that look to come from a trusted source. PhishDetector is a final-year project designed to help internet users authenticate URL links. The system combines Machine Learning, Heuristic Rules, and Visual Analysis to provide a comprehensive Risk Score.
